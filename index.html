Executive Summary

The National Tracer Commodity Dashboard provides real-time visibility of the availability, stock status, and reporting performance of essential tracer medicines and medical commodities across health facilities in Zambia. By consolidating weekly reports from facilities, districts, and provinces, the dashboard enables timely monitoring of commodity availability, months of stock (MOS), stock imbalances, and reporting compliance throughout the health system.

The dashboard supports evidence-based decision-making by highlighting stock-outs, emergency stock levels, understocking, overstocking, and excess stock, thereby facilitating timely redistribution, resupply, and corrective interventions. It also enables trend analysis over time, allowing provinces and national stakeholders to monitor performance, identify emerging supply risks, strengthen data quality, and track improvements in commodity security.

Through enhanced visibility and accountability, the National Tracer Commodity Dashboard strengthens supply chain performance, supports continuity of health services, reduces the risk of commodity shortages and wastage, and contributes to equitable access to essential medicines and health commodities across Zambia.
